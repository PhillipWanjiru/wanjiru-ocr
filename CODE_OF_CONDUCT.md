# Code of conduct

This is a short, plain code of conduct written for a small project. If the project grows, the maintainer may adopt
the Contributor Covenant in its place, and will say so in the changelog.

## What we ask

- Be respectful. Disagree about the work, never about the person.
- Be exact. Say what you know, what you assume and what you have not checked. A claim without its evidence is a
  guess, and a guess is welcome when it is labelled as one.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Welcome newcomers. Explain a rule when you apply it.
- Respect privacy. Do not share other people's personal data or documents, in any form, on any channel of this project.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, insults, or demeaning remarks about anyone's person, background or identity.
- Publishing someone's private information, or a real identity document, bank detail or invoice.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Misrepresenting the project's results: presenting a measurement on fictional data as a result on real documents,
  or a number without its interval, machine and method.

## Where this applies

The repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer: **[contact to be set by the owner before the repository is made public]**. Reports are
kept confidential. The maintainer decides what follows, which can be a reminder, a warning, a temporary or permanent
removal from the project's spaces, and says why. A person affected by a decision can ask for it to be looked at again by someone
who was not involved, when there is such a person.

The maintainer is bound by this code like everyone else. A report about the maintainer goes to
**[a second contact, to be named by the owner]**.
