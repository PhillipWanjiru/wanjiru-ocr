# Dedication

*This page is yours to write. It is left empty on purpose.*

The project carries your mother's name because you wanted it to remind you, and anyone who reads it, of what
matters most. The words that go on this page have to be yours: nobody else knows her, and nothing written by
someone else could stand here without being less than she is.

A few questions, only if they help. None of them needs an answer, and none of them needs to appear on the
page.

- Who is she to you, in a sentence you would say aloud?
- What is it about her that you think of when the work gets hard?
- If a stranger read only this page of the repository, what would you want them to know?
- Is there something she said, in her own language, that belongs here as she said it?
- Who is this page for: her, you, or someone who will read it later?

When you have written it, put it below this line and delete the rest of the page. Until then, the project's name
is the dedication, and this file is the place that is kept for it.
