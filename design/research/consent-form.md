# Consent form for research sessions

**This is a template.** Fill the square brackets, and have it read against the law that applies where
the participant lives before using it (for example the Data Protection Act, 2019, of Kenya, or the GDPR for
participants in the European Union). It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

It is for interviews and contextual inquiries (`plan.md`). Collecting documents for the golden set needs
its own form, which this one can be adapted to (`data/datasheets/golden-v0.md`, section 3).

---

## Research on how documents are processed at work

**Who is asking.** [Name of the researcher], [role], for the Wanjiru System project. Contact:
[email address and telephone number].

**What the research is about.** We are learning how people in accounting, bookkeeping and identity checking
get the contents of invoices, receipts and identity documents into their systems, so that we can design software
that helps with that work. We are interested in your experience, not in testing you.

**What taking part involves.** One conversation of about 45 to 60 minutes, and for some people, a visit of 60
to 90 minutes in which we watch you work as you normally do and ask about it. We will ask about your work, the
tools you use, and what goes right and wrong.

**What we ask you not to share.** Please do not show or read out personal or confidential information about
your customers, suppliers or colleagues that you are not allowed to share. We do not need the contents of any
document. If one is on the screen during a visit, we will not photograph it or write down the values on it.

**What we record, and why.** With your permission, we record the audio of the conversation so that we can
listen again instead of relying on memory. We also write notes. We do not record video.

**How we protect what you tell us.**

- Your name is replaced by a code (such as P03) in all notes and transcripts. The list that links codes to
  names is stored separately from the notes.
- Recordings and notes are stored encrypted, and only the researcher [and named colleagues] can open them.
- When we quote you, we remove anything that could identify you, your employer or your customers.
- We keep the recordings until the analysis is finished and no longer than [12 months], then delete them.
  Anonymised notes may be kept longer.
- We do not use what you tell us for anything other than designing this software, and we do not sell or pass
  it to anyone else.

**Your choices.**

- Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question, stop at any time, and ask us to stop recording.
- You can change your mind after the session. Up to 14 days afterwards you can ask us to delete everything
  we collected from you, by contacting [the researcher], and we will do it and confirm it.
- Saying no, or stopping, has no consequences of any kind for you.

**Benefits and risks.** There is no direct benefit to you apart from [a thank-you of ... / no payment].
The risk is small: you might feel uncomfortable talking about mistakes at work. You do not have to.

**Questions or complaints.** Ask the researcher at any time. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you may
contact [the data protection authority that applies].
